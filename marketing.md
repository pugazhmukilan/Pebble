# Pebble App Marketing

## Tagline

**Track what matters, keep it simple.**

## Short Description

Manage personal expenses locally and privately with a clean, no‑auth, no‑cloud Flutter app.

## Full Description

Pebble is a lightweight Flutter app designed for users who want a straightforward way to record and visualize their personal finances, without any data leaving the device. It features:

- Manual, offline bookkeeping – no server or external accounts.
- Local, encrypted storage via Hive for data privacy.
- Intuitive expense categories, allowing quick categorisation.
- Simple visualisations (charts, summaries) that fit both mobile and desktop.
- No authentication or cloud sync – everything is stored on-device.
- Clean UI built with the Inter font, minimalistic design.

With Pebble you can focus on your finances without the complexity or privacy concerns of typical online budget trackers.
